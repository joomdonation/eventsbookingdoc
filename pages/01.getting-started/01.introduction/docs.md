**Events Booking** is a Joomla extension for event registration. Create free and paid events, accept individual and group registrations, and process payments through online gateways such as PayPal, Authorize.net, Eway, WorldPay, Stripe, Mollie, 2Checkout, and First Data. For more information, visit [https://joomdonation.com/joomla-extensions/events-booking-joomla-events-registration.html](https://joomdonation.com/joomla-extensions/events-booking-joomla-events-registration.html).
