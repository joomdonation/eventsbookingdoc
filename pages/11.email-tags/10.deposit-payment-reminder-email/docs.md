title: 'Remaining Payment Reminder Email'
visible: true
---

This email reminds registrants who paid a deposit to pay their remaining balance.

To edit the reminder, go to Events Booking -> Emails & Messages and open **Remaining Payment Messages**. Edit **Deposit Payment Reminder Email Subject** and **Deposit Payment Reminder Email Body**.

## Supported Tags

- Any tags listed in [Common Tags](/email-tags/common-tags)
- [AMOUNT]: The remaining payment amount which users have to pay to complete payment for registration
- [DEPOSIT_PAYMENT_LINK] : The link which users can click on to go to a form to complete the payment.
