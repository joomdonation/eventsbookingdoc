---
title: 'Waiting List Email'
visible: true
---

Events Booking emails the user and administrator when someone joins the waiting list for a full event. You can use all tags listed in [Common Tags](/email-tags/common-tags).

You can find the email settings on the **Waiting List Messages** tab.

- The email subject and message sent to user can be changed via **Waiting List Confirmation Email Subject**  and **Waiting List Confirmation Email Body** items
- The email subject and message sent to administrator can be changed via **Waiting List Notification Email Subject**  and **Waiting List Notification Email Body** items.
