---
title: 'Offline Payment Reminder Email'
published: true
---

This email is sent to registrants who use offline payment for registration to remind them to pay for the registration (for example, send payment to admin via bank transfer). It is sent by **Events Booking - Offline Payment Handle** plugin.

The subject and message of the email can be configured in **Reminder Messages** tab: **Offline Payment Reminder Email Subject** and **Offline Payment Reminder Email Body**


You can use any tag listed in [Common Tags](/email-tags/common-tags) in this email.
