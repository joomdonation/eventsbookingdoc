---

Events Booking emails registrants when an administrator cancels their event.

## Configure Email Subject and Email Body
To edit the email, go to Events Booking -> Emails & Messages and open **Registration Email Messages**. Edit **Event Cancel Email Subject** and **Event Cancel Email Body**.

## Supported Tags
You can use any tags listed in [Common Tags](/email-tags/common-tags)
