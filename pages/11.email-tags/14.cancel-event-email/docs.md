This is the email sent to registrants when admin cancel an event.

## Configure Email Subject and Email Body
The email subject and email body of the email can be configured in Events Booking -> Emails & Messages, in **Registration Email Messages** tab. It's **Event Cancel Email Subject** and **Event Cancel Email Body**

## Supported Tags
You can use any tags listed in [Common Tags](/email-tags/common-tags)
