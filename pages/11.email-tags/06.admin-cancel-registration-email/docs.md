---
title: 'Admin Cancel Registration Email'
published: true
---

This is the email sent to registrant when an admin/manager cancel his registration from registrants management.

Go to Events Booking -> Emails & Messages, look at **Cancel Registration Messages** tab, find and change the two messages below: **User Registration Cancelled Email Subject**, **User Registration Cancelled Email Body** to meet your need

- Supported Tags:

Any tags listed in [Common Tags](/email-tags/common-tags) documentation.
