---
title: 'Frontend Management'
---

Give authorized users the tools to manage registrants, events, or locations from the frontend of your site.

- [Manage Registrants](/frontend-management/manage-registrants)
- [Export Registrants](/frontend-management/export-registrants)
- [Mass Mail](/frontend-management/mass-mail) — Email all registrants for an event.
- [Manage Events](/frontend-management/manage-events) — Let users view and manage their own events.
- [Submit Event](/frontend-management/submit-event) — Provide a form for users to submit an event.
- [Manage Locations](/frontend-management/manage-locations) — Let users view and manage their locations.
- [Submit Location](/frontend-management/submit-location) — Provide a form for users to submit a location.
