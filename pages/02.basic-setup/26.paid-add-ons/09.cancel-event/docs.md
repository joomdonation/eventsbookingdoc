---
title: 'Cancel Event'
published: true
---

Sometime, you would need to cancel your events and Events Booking supports this option. To cancel an event:

1. Go to **Events Booking** -> **Emails & Messages**, look at **Registration Email Messages** tab, configure subject and body of the email:

- Event Cancel Email Subject
- Event Cancel Email Body

2. Go to **Events Booking** -> **Events**, check on the checkbox next to the events you want to cancel,  and press **Actions -> Cancel** button in the toolbar.

When you do that, the system will:

- Update status of all registrants of that event to Cancelled
- Update status of the event to Cancelled and don't allow registration anymore.
- Send an email to all registrants of that event to inform them that the event is cancelled.
