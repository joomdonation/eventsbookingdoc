If you use [Certificates Feature](/advanced-features/certificates), you might want to send certificates manually to your registrants from Registrants Management screen.

To send certificates to selected registrants:

- Configure the email messages as described at [Certificate Email](/email-tags/certificate-email)
- Check on the checkbox next to the registration records which you want to send certificate to
- Press **Actions -> Send Certificates** button in the toolbar.
