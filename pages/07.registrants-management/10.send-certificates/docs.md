If you use the [Certificates Feature](/advanced-features/certificates), you can send certificates manually from the Registrants Management screen.

To send certificates to selected registrants:

- Configure the email messages as described at [Certificate Email](/email-tags/certificate-email)
- Select the checkbox next to the registration records which you want to send certificate to
- Press **Actions -> Send Certificates** button in the toolbar.
