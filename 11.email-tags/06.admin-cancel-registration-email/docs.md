---
title: 'Admin Cancel Registration Email'
published: true
---

Events Booking emails the registrant when an administrator or manager cancels their registration from registrants management.

To edit the email, go to Events Booking -> Emails & Messages and open **Cancel Registration Messages**. Edit **User Registration Cancelled Email Subject** and **User Registration Cancelled Email Body**.

- Supported Tags:

Any tags listed in [Common Tags](/email-tags/common-tags) documentation.
