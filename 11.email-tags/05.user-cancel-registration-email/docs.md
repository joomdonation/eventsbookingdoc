---
title: 'User Registration Cancel Email'
published: true
---

Events Booking emails the administrator and registrants when a registrant cancels their registration.

## Email Sent To Administrator

To edit the email, go to Events Booking -> Emails & Messages and open **Cancel Registration Messages**. Edit **Registration Cancel Notification Email Subject** and **Registration Cancel Notification Email Body**.

- Supported Tags:

Any tags listed in [Common Tags](/email-tags/common-tags) documentation

## Email Sent To Registrant

To edit the email, go to Events Booking -> Emails & Messages and open **Cancel Registration Messages**. Edit **Registration Cancel Notification Email Subject** and **Registration Cancel Notification Email Body**.

- Supported Tags:

Any tags listed in [Common Tags](/email-tags/common-tags) documentation

## Waiting List Cancel Email messages

In case someone joined waiting list but cancel their registration, the system will uses a different email messages to send email. These messages can be found in the **Waiting List Messages** tab:

- For email send to administrator, the subject and message of the email is controlled by **Waiting List Cancel Notification Email Subject** and **Waiting List Cancel Notification Email Body**.
- For email send to waiting list users, the subject and message of the email is controlled by **Waiting List Cancel Confirmation Email Subject** and **Waiting List Cancel Confirmation Email Body**.
