Publish Events Booking modules in your Joomla template’s module positions to display events, offer search, or help visitors browse by category or location.

- [Upcoming Events](/modules/upcoming-events)
- [Mini Calendar](/modules/mini-calendar)
- [Map](/modules/events-map) — Show event locations on a map.
- [Categories Slider](/modules/categories-slider)
- [Advanced Events Slider](/modules/advanced-events-slider)
- [Search](/modules/search) — Let visitors search by keyword, category, or location.
- [Categories](/modules/categories) — Let visitors browse events by category.
- [Locations](/modules/locations) — Let visitors browse events by venue.
- [Cities](/modules/cities) — Let visitors browse events by city.
- [States](/modules/states) — Let visitors browse events by state.
- [Cart](/modules/cart) — Show selected events and link to checkout.
