Use Joomla’s task scheduler to automate payment reminders, email registrant lists, and clean up records or generated files.

- [Offline Payment Handle](/scheduled-tasks/offline-payment-handle) — Automate reminders and cleanup for unpaid offline registrations.
- [Deposit Payment Reminder](/scheduled-tasks/deposit-payment-reminder) — Remind registrants to pay the balance after a deposit.
- [Email Registrants List](/scheduled-tasks/email-registrants-list)
- [Delete Incomplete Payment Registrations](/scheduled-tasks/delete-incomplete-payment-registrations)
- [Incomplete Payment Registrations Notification](/scheduled-tasks/incomplete-payment-registrations-notification)
- [Clean Emails Log](/scheduled-tasks/clean-emails-log)
- [Delete Old PDF Invoices](/scheduled-tasks/delete-old-pdf-invoices)
- [Delete Old PDF Tickets](/scheduled-tasks/delete-old-pdf-tickets)
- [Delete Old PDF Certificates](/scheduled-tasks/delete-old-pdf-certificates)
- [Delete Old QRCODEs](/scheduled-tasks/delete-old-qrcodes)
- [Delete Past Events Index](/scheduled-tasks/delete-past-events-index) — Remove past events from Joomla Smart Search results.
- [Delete Orphan Files](/scheduled-tasks/delete-orphan-files) — Remove unused uploads from registration forms.
