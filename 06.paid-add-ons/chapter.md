These add-ons connect Events Booking to Google Calendar, Google Sheets, and electronic invoicing services.

- [Events Booking - Google Calendar](/paid-add-ons/events-booking-google-calendar) — Synchronize events with Google Calendar.
- [Events Booking - Google Spreadsheet](/paid-add-ons/events-booking-google-spreadsheet) — Synchronize registrant data with Google Sheets.
- [Events Booking - E-Invoice](/paid-add-ons/events-booking-einvoice) — Generate electronic invoices alongside PDF invoices.
- [Events Booking - Billit](/paid-add-ons/events-booking-billit) — Generate Peppol-compliant invoices through Billit.
